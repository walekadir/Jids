THE NEWSROOM / MY CURRENT WORK

From first lead to final bulletin.

I write, compile and edit news for radio and web, and cover events worth reporting. Behind every short story is a careful editorial process.

SOURCE → VERIFY → WRITE → REPORT
01 / DISCOVER

Find what matters.

Follow credible leads, public statements and news sources. Look beyond the headline for the real development and its public relevance.

02 / VERIFY

Check before sharing.

Cross-check names, dates, quotes and figures. Confirm claims with primary sources or reliable corroboration; clearly attribute what is still disputed.

03 / WRITE FOR RADIO

Make it easy to hear.

Lead with the verified news, use short speakable sentences, plain words and clear attribution. Read copy aloud: listeners usually hear it only once.

04 / ADAPT FOR WEB

Keep it clear online.

Build a useful headline, add essential context, organise details for scanning, credit sources and update or correct the story when needed.

WHEN I STEP OUT TO REPORT

The fieldwork has its own rhythm.

Good event coverage begins before the first microphone comes on.

BEFORE ↗

Research the subject and speakers. Prepare questions, contacts, notebook, charged recorder, batteries and a backup plan.

ON SITE ↗

Observe closely. Note names, sequences and times; capture accurate quotes and ambient audio, with appropriate consent and care.

AFTER ↗

Review recordings and notes, verify facts, seek missing perspectives, then shape a concise, attributed report for radio or digital readers.

⚖
MY EDITORIAL COMPASS

Fair. Factual. Impartial.

News should inform, not campaign. I separate fact from opinion, avoid sensational or loaded language, seek relevant perspectives and give people a fair chance to respond. My approach reflects the accuracy, balance and impartiality principles of the Nigeria Broadcasting Code and professional journalism ethics.
